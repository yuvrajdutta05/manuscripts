## Papers

### Fluctuations in p-spin Ising Model

**Yuvraj Dutta and Sumit Mukherjee**

[Read PDF](p_spin_ising.pdf) · [Main results and theorems](p_spin_ising/README.md)

We study fluctuations of the global magnetization in $p$-spin Ising models ($p\geq 3$) with general symmetric interaction tensors and an external magnetic field. In a Dobrushin-type high-temperature regime, we establish an explicit Berry–Esseen bound in terms of the interaction tensor's pair marginal, recovering the Gaussian limit and $N^{-1/2}$ rate of the $p$-spin Curie–Weiss model under suitable regularity conditions. We also obtain exponential concentration, a law of large numbers, comparison bounds with a product measure, and quantitative mean-field approximations to the log-partition function.

### Entropic Matching for Filtering and Smoothing of Stochastic Differential Equations

**Yannick Eich, Yuvraj Dutta, and Heinz Koeppl**

[Read PDF](f%26s_sdes.pdf) · [Model and main results](f%26s_sdes/README.md)

We develop an entropic-matching framework for approximate Bayesian filtering and smoothing of stochastic differential equations, using lognormal and gamma assumed-density approximations to handle positive, potentially skewed latent states. The approach derives finite-dimensional parameter dynamics, incorporates expectation propagation for non-conjugate observation models, and evaluates the resulting approximations against sequential Monte Carlo references on benchmark models from finance and population biology.
